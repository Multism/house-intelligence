Frozen (Victor Calderone Extended Club Mix) · Madonna · New York, 1998

Calderone turns a dark ballad into a tribal cathedral: cascading toms, rolling percussion, long builds and dramatic breakdowns. Madonna's voice becomes an incantation over a carpet of drums.

Late-90s Manhattan: Twilo, Roxy, Sound Factory and the circuit parties, a post-AIDS culture of the body, the night and collective survival. Madonna had borrowed vogue from ballroom in 1990; here the loop closes.
