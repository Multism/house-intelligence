Show Me Love (StoneBridge Club Mix) · Robin S · New York, 1993

The original came out on Champion in 1990 and flopped. Swedish remixer StoneBridge kept only the vocal and the kick drum. Moving to the next preset on his Korg M1, "Organ 2", he found the organ bass that became one of the sounds of the 90s.

Released in 1993, the remix reached number 6 in the UK, number 5 in the US and number one on US club charts. It has been sampled or reworked for thirty years, from Jason Derulo to Charli XCX and Beyoncé's "Break My Soul".
