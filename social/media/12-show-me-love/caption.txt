Show Me Love (StoneBridge Club Mix) · Robin S · New York, 1992

Swedish remixer StoneBridge threw out the original production and kept the voice: an organ bass from a Korg M1 preset, a dry kick, and Robin S soaring over it. That M1 organ became one of the sounds of the 90s.

The remix went global in 1993 and has been sampled and remixed for thirty years since. On gay dancefloors and in ballroom, it is the gospel diva voice par excellence.
