Love Is the Message · MFSB · Philadelphia, 1973

A message for today, and the record where it all begins.

Gamble & Huff's studio orchestra: silky strings, horns and a bass-and-drums groove that stands on its own. The long instrumental break became DJs' raw material, looped on two decks for minutes on end. That loop, as a place of collective trance, is what house is made of.

An anthem of David Mancuso's Loft and of 1970s New York's Black and Latino gay dancefloors, where its promise of universal love became the dancefloor's own.
