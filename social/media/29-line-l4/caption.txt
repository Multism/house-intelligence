Sound systems → Shoom & Haçienda → Bleep → Hardcore & Jungle → UK Garage → Dubstep → Chart house

Before acid house, Britain danced to Jamaican sound systems and jazz-funk all-dayers. It kept that cult of sub-bass through every mutation: bleep in Sheffield, jungle, 2-step, dubstep in Croydon, and in 2013 house at the top of the charts.
