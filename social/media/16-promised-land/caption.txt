Promised Land · Joe Smooth · Chicago, 1987

The last record of the year: luminous piano chords, a gospel progression and a lyric of hope. House as a civil-rights hymn.

Joe Smooth, a Chicago DJ schooled in soul and gospel, wrote a political anthem in a genre said to be apolitical. Then the "promised land" of a Black and queer scene became the utopia of British ravers in 1988 and 1989, a transfer of meaning at the heart of house history.
