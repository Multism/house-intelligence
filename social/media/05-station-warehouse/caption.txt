The Warehouse · Chicago · 1977–1982

Frankie Knuckles played here from 1977 until November 1982, when he left for the Power Plant. The crowd was mostly Black and gay, and the night ran until the morning.

Where does the word "house" come from? Three versions still compete: a sign Knuckles saw, the "As heard at the Warehouse" bins at the Importes Etc. record shop, or simply a generic name for that kind of disco.
