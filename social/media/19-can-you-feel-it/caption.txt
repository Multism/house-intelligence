Can You Feel It · Mr. Fingers · Chicago, 1986

Warm pads, a round bassline and almost no variation for seven minutes. Depth comes from restraint. This is the template for all deep house.

Larry Heard was a jazz-fusion drummer, and he composes as a harmonist, with sevenths and ninths. Later versions carry Chuck Roberts's sermon: "In the beginning there was Jack." House as a feeling, a theology of belonging born in Black gay clubs.
