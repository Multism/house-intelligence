Your Love · Frankie Knuckles presents Jamie Principle · Chicago, 1987

An endlessly cycling arpeggio, a minimal bassline and an almost whispered vocal. Jamie Principle wrote it at home; Frankie Knuckles played it from a reel-to-reel tape at the Power Plant for years before it was ever released.

That is how Chicago worked: the DJ is the first broadcaster, the record comes later.

Knuckles, the Godfather of House, was a Black gay DJ from the Bronx and the Continental Baths. "Your Love" is one of the founding love songs of queer house.

120 BPM · 1A · Energy 6/10
