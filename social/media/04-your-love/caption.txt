Your Love · Jamie Principle · Chicago, 1986

An endlessly cycling arpeggio, a minimal bassline and an almost whispered vocal. Jamie Principle wrote it; Frankie Knuckles played it from tape in clubs for over a year before it came out on Persona in April 1986. Trax reissued it in 1987, credited to Knuckles.

That is how Chicago worked: the DJ is the first broadcaster, the record comes later.

Frankie Knuckles, the Godfather of House, was a Black gay DJ. "Your Love" is one of the founding love songs of queer house.
