Paradise Garage · New York · 1977–1987

At 84 King Street, Larry Levan turned the dancefloor into a church for a mostly gay Black and Latino crowd: soul, gospel, dub effects and a legendary sound system.

Garage house took its name from this room.

Listen: Love Is the Message · Love Sensation · Is It All Over My Face · Don't Make Me Wait
