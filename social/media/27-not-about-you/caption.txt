Not About You · Honey Dijon feat. Hadiya George · Chicago / New York, 2022

Bright vocal house, a round bassline and the soul voice of Atlanta singer Hadiya George: Chicago house rewritten for 2022. BBC Radio 1 playlisted it before the album Black Girl Magic came out on 18 November 2022.

Honey Dijon discovered house at 12. She co-produced Black Girl Magic with Luke Solomon and Chris Penny, and produced two tracks on Beyoncé's Renaissance. She says she wants to "keep this culture in the conversation": to reground house in the Black and queer communities that made it.

124 BPM · 8B · Energy 7/10
