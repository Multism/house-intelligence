Ballroom & Voguing · 1920s–

Ballroom predates house: its roots go back to the Harlem Renaissance. "Old Way" took hold in the 1980s (House of LaBeija, House of Corey), "New Way" in the early 1990s.

The link with house came through the Sound Factory (1989–1995) and The Ha Dance (1991): every "ha" calls for a pose.
