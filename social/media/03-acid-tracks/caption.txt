Acid Tracks · Phuture · Chicago, 1987

DJ Pierre, Earl "Spanky" Smith Jr. and Herb J got hold of a second-hand Roland TB-303 without knowing how to use it. The accident became the method: Spanky programmed, Pierre twisted the cutoff and resonance until that sound appeared.

Ron Hardy played the tape several times in one night at the Music Box. The crowd rejected it, then demanded it. The name "acid" came from a rumour that the DJ was spiking the water.

Acid was born in a Black and gay club. Once it crossed the Atlantic, the smiley partly erased where it came from.
