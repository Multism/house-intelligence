Move Your Body (The House Music Anthem) · Marshall Jefferson · Chicago, 1986

Hammered piano chords over a rigid drum-machine pattern, an octave bassline and Curtis McClain's church-born vocal. The birth certificate of piano house.

Jefferson says he played the bassline at 40 BPM, then sped it up to 122. Ron Hardy played it at the Music Box, Frankie Knuckles at the Power Plant. "Gotta have house music, all night long": for a Black, gay, working-class crowd, a secular church.

122 BPM · 8B · Energy 8/10
