Move Your Body (The House Music Anthem) · Marshall Jefferson · Chicago, 1986

Hammered piano chords over a rigid drum-machine pattern, an octave bassline and Curtis McClain's church-born vocal. The birth certificate of piano house.

Jefferson worked at the post office and bought his machines on credit, building tracks part by part. Ron Hardy played it at the Music Box, Frankie Knuckles at the Power Plant. "Gotta have house music, all night long": for a Black, gay, working-class crowd, a secular church.
