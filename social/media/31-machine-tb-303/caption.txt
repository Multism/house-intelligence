Roland TB-303 · 1981

Roland made it for guitarists rehearsing alone. It sounded nothing like an electric bass, flopped, and was discontinued within a few years.

In Chicago, Phuture twisted the cutoff, resonance, accent and slide live. The bass started to "talk", and acid house was born from that accident. Its programming is so counter-intuitive that many famous patterns came from mistakes.

Heard on: Acid Tracks · Voodoo Ray · Da Funk
