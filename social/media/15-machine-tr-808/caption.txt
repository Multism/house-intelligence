Roland TR-808 · 1980

Roland wanted a programmable, "realistic" drum kit to back musicians in rehearsal. It sounded like no real drums and sold poorly.

Resold cheap on the second-hand market, it fell into the hands of New York electro, hip-hop and then Chicago's first house producers. Its kick, a deep sine wave with a long decay, doubles as a bassline: the 808 sub.

Heard on: On and On · Don't Make Me Wait · Voodoo Ray · Yo Yo Get Funky!

Discontinued after a few years, it became one of the most sampled machines in history.
