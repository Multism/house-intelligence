Da Funk · Daft Punk · Paris, 1995

A distorted, filtered synth-bass riff halfway between funk and acid, over a compressed 909. At 111 BPM the groove is heavy, closer to G-funk than to Chicago.

It first came out on Soma, Slam's Glasgow label, before Virgin signed the duo. The French Touch was recognised in London before Paris. And the Paris scene crystallised at the Respect nights at Queen, a gay club on the Champs-Élysées; their tour was called "Respect Is Burning".
