Music Sounds Better With You · Stardust · Paris, 1998

A loop from Chaka Khan's "Fate" (1981) runs through a filter that opens and closes over a four-to-the-floor kick, with Benjamin Diamond singing on top. The whole track rests on the filter's breathing.

Thomas Bangalter, Alan Braxe and Benjamin Diamond released only one record under this name. Filter house turned the disco of 1970s Black and gay dancefloors into raw material for a wider audience: homage or appropriation, depending on how you hear it.
