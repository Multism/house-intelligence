The Ha Dance · Masters At Work · New York, 1991

A "ha!" sampled from Trading Places, a crash cymbal, a nervy rhythm. Louie Vega and Kenny "Dope" Gonzalez made an instrumental; ballroom made it a choreography. The crash on the fourth beat became the cue for vogue femme's dip.

The sample comes from a scene full of blackface and slurs. Black and Latina, queer and trans ballroom turned it around, and has been reissuing and remixing it for more than thirty years.

122 BPM · 4A · SP-1200 + MPC60
