Voodoo Ray · A Guy Called Gerald · Manchester, 1988

Gerald Simpson, a young Black Mancunian, made it in his bedroom on second-hand gear: a more melodic 303 than Phuture's, a light 808 groove and a voice chopped into abstraction. Proof that house had become a language others could speak.

An anthem of 1988's Second Summer of Love: the Haçienda, warehouse parties, then the orbital raves around the M25, until the 1994 Criminal Justice Act.
