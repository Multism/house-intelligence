Gypsy Woman (She's Homeless) · Crystal Waters · Baltimore, 1991

An organ riff, a dry kick and the famous "la da dee la dee da". Crystal Waters, a poet and civil servant, wrote a song about a homeless woman, and the Basement Boys produced it in the wake of New York garage.

A worldwide hit about urban poverty over a euphoric groove. In ballroom, the "la da dee" became a vogue classic, reused in remix after remix.
