Gypsy Woman (She's Homeless) · Crystal Waters · Baltimore, 1991

A keyboard riff, a heavy bassline and the famous scatted "la da dee, la da da". The song portrays a homeless woman who wears make-up and sees herself as beautiful while busking.

Crystal Waters had written poetry since childhood and worked for the District of Columbia government. She wrote the lyric with Neal Conway, inspired by a woman she saw outside the Mayflower Hotel, and Baltimore's Basement Boys produced it. Number 2 in the UK, number 8 in the US, number one on US club charts: a dancefloor hit about the street.

122 BPM · 11A · Energy 7/10
