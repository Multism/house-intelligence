Basta Now: Women, Trans & Non-binary in Experimental Music · Fanny Chiarello · Permanent Draft · 2025

French poet and novelist Fanny Chiarello maps 3,867 women, trans and non-binary artists in experimental music, with discographies. Expanded second edition (482 pages, in English), published by Permanent Draft, the label and micro-press she founded with drummer Valentina Magaletti.

“What interests me in this book is that it doesn't simply set out to restore overlooked artists, but to offer another map of music history, outside the usual male canon. It's not a book about music: you can use it as a tool for musical exploration.”
