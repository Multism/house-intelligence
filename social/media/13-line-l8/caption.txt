Line 8 · Africas

Afro House → Gqom → Amapiano

South African house was born in Hillbrow and Pretoria in the late 1980s, next to kwaito. In Durban, gqom broke the kick and travelled by taxi and WhatsApp. In Gauteng, amapiano slowed everything down and gave the melody to the log drum.

House there is mass popular music, not a niche.
