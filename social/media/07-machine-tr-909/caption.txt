Roland TR-909 · 1983

Roland built it to succeed the 808, with sampled cymbals and MIDI sync. At launch it was judged too expensive and not realistic enough, and it was sold off cheap.

That is how it reached Detroit and Chicago. Its punchy kick became the unit of measure of house and techno, and its open hi-hat defined the off-beat of the dancefloor.

Heard on: Strings of Life · Can You Feel It · Big Fun · Da Funk · Frozen (Victor Calderone)

In The Booth, the pulse plays each record at its exact tempo with a 909 kick.
