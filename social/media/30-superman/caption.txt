Superman · Black Coffee feat. Bucie · Durban / Johannesburg, 2009

Woody, polyrhythmic percussion, melancholic pads and Bucie's voice. The groove is restrained, almost deep, but it breathes differently from American four-to-the-floor.

Nathi "Black Coffee" Maphumulo grew up in Umlazi. In 1990 he was injured when a car ploughed into a crowd celebrating Mandela's release, and lost most of the use of his left arm. He learned to mix one-handed. In South Africa, this was the hit of a generation.

120 BPM · 3A · Energy 5/10
