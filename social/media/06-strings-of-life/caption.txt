Strings of Life · Rhythim Is Rhythim · Detroit, 1987

Sampled strings and a piano improvisation by Michael James, cut into syncopated riffs over a relentless 909. No conventional bassline, so the tension never resolves: an orchestra in free fall.

Derrick May saw the machine as a way out of a city the car industry had left behind. In Europe, the record became a rave anthem.

The techno story often forgets its queer debts, starting with Ken Collier, the Black gay Detroit DJ whose sets schooled a whole generation.

125 BPM · 4B · Energy 9/10
