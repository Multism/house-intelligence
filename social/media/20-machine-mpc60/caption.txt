Akai MPC60 · 1988

Designed with Roger Linn as a complete studio in a box: sampling, sequencing and playable pads.

The velocity-sensitive pads and Linn's quantise swing gave loops a human groove. Hip-hop seized it first, then New York garage and house producers who played samples like percussion. Later, UK garage.

Heard on: The Ha Dance (Masters At Work, 1991)

"MPC swing" is now a setting of its own in modern music software.
