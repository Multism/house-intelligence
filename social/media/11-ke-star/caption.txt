Ke Star · Focalistic & Vigro Deep · Pretoria, 2020

The log drum, a percussive bass with a signature glide, carries the melody. Constant shakers, a discreet kick, Focalistic's Sepitori flow floating on top. The groove is horizontal.

Vigro Deep, a teenage producer from Mabopane, belongs to the generation that shaped amapiano on FL Studio in Gauteng's townships. Born at neighbourhood parties, in taxis and taverns, the genre was pushed across the continent by the 2020 lockdown.

Forty years after Chicago, the same question: who holds the keys to house? Black youth, this time in Africa.
