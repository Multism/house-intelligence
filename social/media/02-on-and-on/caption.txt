On and On · Jesse Saunders · Chicago, 1984

The record most often cited as the first house 12" ever pressed. A looped TR-808, a bouncing synth bass, a vocal pared down to a mantra.

The story is better than the music: Saunders rebuilt from memory a bootleg edit he used to play out and that had been stolen from him. House begins as a home-made copy of a vanished record.

The template was set: a DJ, a second-hand machine, an independent pressing (Larry Sherman's plant, the future home of Trax) and a straight line to the local record shops.

120 BPM · 5A · Energy 6/10
