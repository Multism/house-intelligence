Disco / The Loft

L1 · Chicago & Acid

In 1970 David Mancuso opened the Loft in New York: private, invitation-only parties where sound, selection and the whole night became an art. Tom Moulton invented the dance remix, the breakdown and the 12" format (acetates from 1974); in 1976 Salsoul released the first 12" sold to the public, "Ten Percent".
