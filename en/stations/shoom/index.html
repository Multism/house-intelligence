1987–1989

Shoom & Haçienda

L4 · UK Sound System & Bass

In Manchester, Mike Pickering's Nude night played house from 1986; the acid night Hot (Pickering and Jon Dasilva) followed in 1988 (July or November depending on sources). In London, Danny Rampling opened Shoom in autumn 1987 after returning from Ibiza, for some 300 people. Chicago acid became the Second Summer of Love.

Transfer · Acid House ↔ Shoom & Haçienda

From 1986 onwards, Chicago house records circulated through British clubs. London's Shoom and Manchester's Haçienda became major spaces for their dissemination and reinterpretation. The encounter between American acid house, Balearic culture and local club practices contributed to the emergence of British rave culture.

Transfer · Ibiza ↔ Shoom

In 1987 Paul Oakenfold, Danny Rampling and friends discovered Alfredo's eclecticism at Amnesia in Ibiza. Back in London they recreated the balearic spirit at small parties (Shoom, Spectrum) that lit the fuse of the Second Summer of Love.
