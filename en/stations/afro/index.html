Afro House (South Africa)

L8 · Africas

South African house was born in Hillbrow and Pretoria in the late 1980s and coexisted with kwaito in the 1990s. Black Coffee founded Soulistic Music in 2005: afro house became an industry. Osunlade's American spiritual house (Yoruba Records) belongs to another story.

Transfer · Deep & gospel ↔ South African house (imported influence)

An influence, not a lineage: New York and New Jersey deep house reached South Africa through records and radio, and mixed there with kwaito. Resident Advisor describes amapiano as a composite of New York-style deep house, R&B and kwaito.
