Deep House (Mr. Fingers)

L3 · Deep & gospel

Larry Heard, a former jazz-fusion drummer, brought seventh chords, warm pads and a held-back tempo to house. Deep house fed New Jersey's gospel garage; its influence on South African house is one imported influence among others, not a direct lineage.
