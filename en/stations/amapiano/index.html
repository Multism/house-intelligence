Amapiano (Log Drum)

L8 · Africas

Born in the mid-2010s in Gauteng, amapiano blends kwaito, bacardi percussion, jazz, deep house synths and the log drum, a percussive bass most often produced on FL Studio, software shared across the townships. It spread worldwide from 2020.
