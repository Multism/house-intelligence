Gqom (Durban)

L8 · Africas

In Durban, a raw house of heavy, broken kicks circulated through taxis and WhatsApp, then spread via European labels (Gqom Oh!'s The Sound of Durban Vol. 1 compilation in January 2016, Goon Club Allstars) in the mid-2010s. In London, Scratcha DVA says he found in gqom the same energy as the darker side of UK funky.
