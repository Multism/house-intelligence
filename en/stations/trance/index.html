Trance

L9 · Techno & Europe

Trance came out of Frankfurt techno: Mixmag describes the Omen (1988) as the place where techno mutated into trance. Arpeggios, builds and euphoric breakdowns then spread to British and Ibizan clubs, up to the big Dutch gatherings (the 2005 cut-off is that of the book Hypnotised).
