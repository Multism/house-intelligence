Tech House (Wiggle London)

L5 · European Stopovers

The Wiggle parties started in March 1994 (Terry Francis, Nathan Coles, Eddie Richards); Heart & Soul and The End were London's other cradles. Techno's rigour met house's warmth there, before tech house became the sound of Ibiza's megaclubs and then of festivals.
