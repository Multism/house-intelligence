French Touch

L5 · European Stopovers

Paris filtered disco and looped it to ecstasy (Daft Punk, Cassius, Stardust). The "French Touch" label was coined by the British press around 1996; Laurent Garnier and St Germain are precursors or neighbours, more techno or more jazz. The bubble burst in the early 2000s, before a second, electro wave (Ed Banger, Justice) in 2007.
