Paradise Garage

L2 · NYC, Garage & Tribal

At 84 King Street, Larry Levan turned the dancefloor into a church for a mostly gay Black and Latino crowd: soul, gospel, dub effects and a legendary sound system. The word "garage" comes from here.

Transfer · The Warehouse ↔ Paradise Garage

Frankie Knuckles and Larry Levan were two New York friends who came up together in the disco scene (notably at the Continental Baths). In 1977 Knuckles left for Chicago and the Warehouse, while Levan took over the Paradise Garage. Both clubs come from the same school: house and garage are first cousins.
