Ballroom & Voguing

L2 · NYC, Garage & TribalL7 · Ballroom & queer

Ballroom predates house: its origins go back to the Harlem Renaissance. "Old Way" took hold in the 1980s (House of LaBeija, House of Corey), "New Way" in the early 1990s. The link with house came with the Sound Factory (1989–1995) and "The Ha Dance" (1991): every "Ha" calls for a pose.
