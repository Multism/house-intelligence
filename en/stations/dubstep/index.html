Dark Garage / Dubstep

L4 · UK Sound System & Bass

Born around 2000 out of dark garage (Zed Bias, El-B, Horsepower Productions) in Croydon and south London. The FWD>> and DMZ nights were the key venues; "Midnight Request Line" (Skream, 2005) opened the scene to the world, before the "brostep" turn around 2011.
