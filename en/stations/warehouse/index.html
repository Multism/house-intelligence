The Warehouse

L1 · Chicago & Acid

The Chicago club where Frankie Knuckles played from 1977 to November 1982 (when he left for the Power Plant) to a mostly Black and gay crowd. The origin of the word "house" circulates in three competing versions: a sign Knuckles saw, the "As heard at the Warehouse" bins at the Importes Etc. record shop, or a generic term for disco.

Transfer · The Warehouse ↔ Paradise Garage

Frankie Knuckles and Larry Levan were two New York friends who came up together in the disco scene (notably at the Continental Baths). In 1977 Knuckles left for Chicago and the Warehouse, while Levan took over the Paradise Garage. Both clubs come from the same school: house and garage are first cousins.
