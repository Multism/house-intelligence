UK Bleep

L4 · UK Sound System & Bass

In Sheffield, Leeds and Bradford, producers raised on Detroit techno and sound systems invented bleep: pure tones and enormous sub-bass. Warp was born in Sheffield in 1989. Britain's first bass music.

Transfer · UK Bleep ↔ Detroit

In Sheffield and Leeds, imported Detroit 12"s met Caribbean sound-system culture. British producers kept the machine futurism and added monumental sub-bass. Matt Anniss tells this story in Join the Future.
