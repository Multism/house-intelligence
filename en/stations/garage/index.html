Garage House (NYC/NJ)

L2 · NYC, Garage & Tribal

Vocal house from New York and New Jersey: gospel voices, organs, loose grooves. Masters At Work, Strictly Rhythm and Nervous Records made it a global sound, which London would speed up into UK garage.
