Balearic House (Ibiza)

L5 · European Stopovers

Alfredo was DJing at Amnesia from 1984, mixing pop, indie rock and house under the open sky: balearic existed before the British arrived. In September 1987 Oakenfold, Rampling, Holloway and Walker had a revelation there and exported it to London: hence the transfer to Shoom.

Transfer · Ibiza ↔ Shoom

In 1987 Paul Oakenfold, Danny Rampling and friends discovered Alfredo's eclecticism at Amnesia in Ibiza. Back in London they recreated the balearic spirit at small parties (Shoom, Spectrum) that lit the fuse of the Second Summer of Love.
