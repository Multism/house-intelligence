Club Zanzibar (NJ)

L2 · NYC, Garage & TribalL3 · Deep & gospel

In Newark, New Jersey, Zanzibar opened in 1979; Tony Humphries was resident from 1982 to 1994. After the Paradise Garage closed in 1987, the club became a centre of house in the New York area. The "Jersey sound" is gospel, carried notably by Movin' Records (1987).

Transfer · Deep & gospel ↔ South African house (imported influence)

An influence, not a lineage: New York and New Jersey deep house reached South Africa through records and radio, and mixed there with kwaito. Resident Advisor describes amapiano as a composite of New York-style deep house, R&B and kwaito.
