Why this record

At 165 BPM it proves speed doesn't rule out depth.

How it was made

Fast but hushed breakbeats, jazz chords and ambient pads: "atmospheric" drum & bass. Double tempo, deep-house emotion.

Breakbeats (time-stretch)NappesPiano

Origins and production approach

Danny Williamson founded Good Looking Records and championed an intelligent, jazzy jungle in reaction to darkcore's gloom.

Club sociology

Martin James traces how hardcore split around 1993–1994 into jungle and drum & bass, between Caribbean sound systems, pirate radio and raves.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Jungle is a Black British culture fed by reggae and sound systems rather than by queer clubs.

Reference

State of Bass: The Origins of Jungle/Drum & Bass — Martin James (1997). The origins of jungle and drum & bass.
