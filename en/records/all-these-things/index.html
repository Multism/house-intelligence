Why this record

Japanese deep house, open to jazz and Africa.

How it was made

A long album (about seventy-eight minutes) where house appears only at moments: strings, harp, double bass, piano and percussion, Omar Guaindefall's voice and djembe on "Dear African Sky", Josée Hurlock on "You Get Me" and the title track, Henrik Schwarz on "The Session".

Instruments livePianoPercussions

Origins and production approach

Kuniyuki Takahashi is a Japanese producer and a longtime Mule Musiq affiliate. "All These Things" is his second album, first released on CD.

Club sociology

An after-hours record, as critics put it: Japanese deep house moves towards jazz and African and Brazilian music, and Alex From Tokyo reads a poem on it ("Flying Music").
