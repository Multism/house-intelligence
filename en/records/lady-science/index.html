Why this record

The move from deep house to minimal, in a single track.

How it was made

A deep, minimal house track, long and luminous, as its subtitle announces: a sunrise in New York.

SynthésVoix

Origins and production approach

Soul Capsule is the duo of Thomas Melchior and Peter Adshead, aka Baby Ford. Between 1999 and 2001 they released three EPs, including this one on Trelik in 1999.

Club sociology

The track, reissued several times, links American deep house to the European minimal scene organising around Trelik, Playhouse and then Perlon.
