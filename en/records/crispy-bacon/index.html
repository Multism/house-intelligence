Why this record

The France of raves, not only of the filter.

How it was made

A 909 at full throttle, runaway synths and tension that never drops: the techno wing of the French scene, the opposite of the disco filter.

Origins and production approach

Laurent Garnier learned his craft as a DJ at Manchester's Haçienda in the late 80s. Back in France he co-founded F Communications with Éric Morand. He is often counted among the French Touch's precursors rather than at its core: his music remains techno.

Club sociology

In Electrochoc he recounts the crackdown on raves in France, the closures and techno's bad press in the 1990s.

Ballroom & queer scenes

In Paris, house first found its audiences in gay and society clubs such as Le Palace. Garnier inherited that before launching his Wake Up nights at the Rex.

Reference

Electrochoc — Laurent Garnier & David Brun-Lambert (2003). The Haçienda years, then the French techno scene under repression.
