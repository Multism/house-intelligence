Why this record

It proves house can be euphoric and melodic without losing any of its rhythmic grit.

How it was made

Hammered piano chords over a rigid drum-machine pattern, an octave bassline and a church-born vocal from Curtis McClain. This is the birth certificate of piano house: gospel fervour laid over a mechanical grid.

Roland TR-707Piano acoustique (overdub)

Origins and production approach

Jefferson worked at the post office and taught himself the gear, buying machines on credit. No conservatory background: he built tracks part by part and embraced a "non-musician" approach that would become house's default.

Club sociology

Ron Hardy played it at the Music Box, Chicago's rawest club; Frankie Knuckles, at the Power Plant, made it a staple too. The subtitle "The House Music Anthem" is a statement: a scene naming itself and giving itself an anthem.

Ballroom & queer scenes

The Music Box drew a Black, gay, working-class crowd for whom the dancefloor worked like a secular church. The lyrics ("gotta have house music, all night long") turn that dance-bound community into ritual.

Reference

Last Night a DJ Saved My Life — Bill Brewster & Frank Broughton (1999). Chicago chapter: Ron Hardy, the Music Box and the rivalry with Frankie Knuckles.
