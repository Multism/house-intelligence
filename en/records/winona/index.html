Why this record

House becomes its own archive, heard like a memory you never lived.

How it was made

Deep house chords saturated like an old VHS tape, a muffled kick and a vocal sample from a Winona Ryder interview. The degradation is deliberate: it manufactures nostalgia.

Saturation tape / bitcrusherSampler

Origins and production approach

DJ Boring (Tristan Hallis), an Australian-born, London-based producer, belongs to a generation of bedroom producers who learned house on YouTube and use plug-ins to fake the imperfections their elders endured with hardware.

Club sociology

Lo-fi house spread in 2016–2017 through YouTube channels and the algorithm more than through clubs. It was first heard on headphones, only later on a dancefloor.

Ballroom & queer scenes

The aesthetics of memory without the context: lo-fi house quotes Chicago deep house as a texture, without inheriting its communities.

Reference

How Music Works — David Byrne (2012). Sound shaped by where it is heard: headphones, YouTube, the bedroom.
