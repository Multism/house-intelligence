Why this record

The moment dubstep changes its body.

How it was made

A mid-range bassline that twists and growls instead of staying in the sub: the "wobble" that announces dubstep's turn towards the dancefloor.

DAWSub-bass

Origins and production approach

Coki (Dean Harris) formed Digital Mystikz with Mala; with Loefah they founded the DMZ label and night. The "Spongebob / The End" 12" came out in 2007 (DMZ 013).

Club sociology

Caspa & Rusko featured it in their FabricLive mix: it paved the way for the drop-driven dubstep wave, then the brostep of the 2010s.
