Why this record

It opens the Techno & Berlin line.

How it was made

A dry kick, muffled pads and a slow build: Berghain techno, austere and hypnotic.

Boîte à rythmesSynthétiseurs

Origins and production approach

Marcel Dettmann and Ben Klock, Berghain residents, made their first collaboration; it was the first 12" on the Ostgut Ton label (O-Ton 001), launched in late 2005.

Club sociology

Berghain opened in 2004 in a former power station: Berlin became the centre of European techno, in Detroit's lineage.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Berghain and Panorama Bar grew out of Berlin's gay scene (Ostgut, Snax); the queer presence there is structural.

Reference

Rave On: Global Adventures in Electronic Dance Music — Matthew Collin (2018). Berlin after the Wall and Berghain.
