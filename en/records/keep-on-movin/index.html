Why this record

The Sound System station comes alive: British bass before acid.

How it was made

A slow, swaying tempo (the "Funki Dred beat"), a round bassline, strings and Caron Wheeler's voice. The groove comes from the sound system more than from the house club.

Boîtes à rythmesCordesBasse

Origins and production approach

Soul II Soul grew out of a London sound system led by Jazzie B, with Nellee Hooper producing. The collective was a band, a shop and a party all at once.

Club sociology

The collective's Sunday sessions at the Africa Centre in Covent Garden linked Caribbean sound-system culture, soul and emerging house. The single made the UK top 5.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis: this is Black British sound-system culture.
