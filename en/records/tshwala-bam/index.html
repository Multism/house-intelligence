Why this record

The link after Water: amapiano beyond the township.

How it was made

A broad log drum, street chants and a slow tempo: amapiano at its point of maximum circulation.

Log Drum (amapiano)DAW

Origins and production approach

TitoM and Yuppe, Johannesburg producers, made the track with vocals by S.N.E and EeQue. Released in February 2024, it passed 100 million streams in under a month; a remix with Burna Boy followed in May.

Club sociology

After Ke Star and Water, the track confirms amapiano as an industry in itself, spread by viral dances and international DJs.
