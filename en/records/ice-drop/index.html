Why this record

It gives Durban its station on the Africas line.

How it was made

Heavy, off-kilter kicks, dry percussion and almost no melody: gqom runs on rhythmic tension.

DAWKicks

Origins and production approach

DJ Lag (Lwazi Asanda Gwala) wrote the track at 17; it circulated in Durban around 2013 before coming out in late 2016 on an EP released by the London label Goon Club Allstars.

Club sociology

Gqom first circulated through taxis, WhatsApp and township parties, then spread through European labels: the Global South becomes a centre of creation.
