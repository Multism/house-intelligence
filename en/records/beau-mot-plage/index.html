Why this record

Microhouse finds its hit.

How it was made

A five-track 12" ("Cité Grande Terre", "Beau Mot Plage", "Bleu", "Poisson mort", "Keep on Dancin'") in which melodic fragments curl around a light, supple groove.

DAWGuitare (sample)

Origins and production approach

Isolée is Rajko Müller, a German producer born in Frankfurt, released by Playhouse from 1997. "Beau Mot Plage" came out in 1998, then appeared on his album "Rest" (2000).

Club sociology

It is Isolée's biggest hit. Luke Solomon's Classic label picked it up, and it travelled well beyond the minimal scene.
