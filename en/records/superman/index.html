Why this record

It shows house returning to Africa and being rewritten, not imported.

How it was made

Woody, polyrhythmic percussion, melancholic pads and Bucie's voice. The groove is restrained, almost deep, but the rhythm breathes differently from American four-to-the-floor.

DAWPercussions

Origins and production approach

Nathi "Black Coffee" Maphumulo grew up in Umlazi (Durban). In February 1990 a car ploughed into a crowd celebrating Mandela's release in Mthatha: he was left with a brachial plexus injury and a largely paralysed left arm. He learned to mix one-handed, with a very economical approach.

Club sociology

South Africa has been one of the world's biggest house markets since the 90s, through kwaito and radio. House there is mass popular music, not a niche, as this track, the hit of a whole generation, shows.

Ballroom & queer scenes

South African house also has its queer scenes, notably in Johannesburg. Their public visibility is lower than the stars', but they claim their share of the lineage.
