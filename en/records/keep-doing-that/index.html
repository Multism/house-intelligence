Origins and production approach

Omni A.M. is the duo of New York-born Adam Collins and Marky Star, formed in Chicago. Collins co-founded the Euphoria label around 1996; the duo's first vinyl (a green sleeve with a maze) was its second release, in 1997.

Club sociology

A micro-label record, reissued digitally in 2023 with Mark Ambrose remixes: tech house wasn't only a London thing.
