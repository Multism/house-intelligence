Why this record

Reclamation, by those who invented house.

How it was made

Bright vocal house, a round bassline and Hadiya George's soul voice: Chicago house rewritten for 2022.

DAWVoix

Origins and production approach

Honey Dijon, a DJ and producer, discovered house at 12 in her hometown. She co-produced the album Black Girl Magic with Luke Solomon and Chris Penny, and produced two tracks on Beyoncé's Renaissance, also remixing "Break My Soul".

Club sociology

The single with Atlanta singer Hadiya George was playlisted on BBC Radio 1 before the album came out on 18 November 2022: Black and queer house returned to the heart of pop.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Honey Dijon says she wants to "keep this culture in the conversation"; for RA, each of her records tries to reground house in the Black and queer communities that originally upheld it.

Reference

Do You Remember House? Chicago's Queer of Color Undergrounds — Micah E. Salkind (2019). Chicago's Black queer house and its memory.
