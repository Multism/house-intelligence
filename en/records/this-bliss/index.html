Why this record

German minimal turns melodic, almost contemplative.

How it was made

Second album, ten tracks and seventy-four minutes: melodic minimal techno, with "Saturn Strobe" (credited to the composer Howard Skempton), "Walden 2" and "Urlichten", already released on a 12".

DAWSynthés

Origins and production approach

Pantha du Prince is Hendrik Weber, born in 1975 in Bad Wildungen, Germany. A producer and artist, he moved from house and minimal towards what he calls "sonic house", with acoustic instruments and field recordings.

Club sociology

The album reached an audience beyond the clubs. Resident Advisor gave it full marks, then ranked it among the best albums of the decade.
