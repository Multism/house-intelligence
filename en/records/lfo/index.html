Why this record

The Bleep station comes alive, and the transfer to Detroit becomes audible.

How it was made

Pure tones (the "bleeps"), an enormous sub-bass and a dry rhythm: Detroit techno filtered through Yorkshire's sound systems.

SynthétiseursBoîte à rythmes

Origins and production approach

Mark Bell and Gez Varley, two young Leeds producers, signed to Warp, the label founded in Sheffield in 1989.

Club sociology

The record entered the UK top 20: bleep became the first British bass music to reach a mass audience.
