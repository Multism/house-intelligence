Why this record

Jungle as a grand form.

How it was made

Chopped, time-stretched breakbeats, string pads and Diane Charlemagne's soul vocal: jungle as urban symphony.

Breakbeats (time-stretch)CordesVoix soul

Origins and production approach

Goldie, a mixed-race graffiti artist who grew up in children's homes in the Midlands, founded Metalheadz and turned jungle into auteur music.

Club sociology

The single reached number 39 in the UK and number 6 on the dance chart; the album Timeless climbed to number 7 in 1995. Jungle won critical recognition, and Metalheadz's Sunday sessions at the Blue Note in Hoxton began in July 1995.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No queer axis: jungle was built within Black British sound-system culture.
