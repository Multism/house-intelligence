Why this record

It shows how America claimed rave for itself.

How it was made

The "Laura Palmer" theme from Twin Peaks sampled over a rave beat, piano stabs and a voice shouting "go": cinematic techno.

SamplerSynthés (cordes)

Origins and production approach

Moby, a vegan, Christian, sober New York DJ, stood out in rave culture. In Porcelain he recounts poverty, Manhattan's clubs and the 1990s scene.

Club sociology

The single reached number 10 in the UK and number 1 on Music Week's dance and indie charts, while stalling at 18 on Billboard's club chart. Michaelangelo Matos shows America inventing techno, then rediscovering it through Europe.

Ballroom & queer scenes

In Porcelain, Moby describes New York clubs (Mars, Limelight) as mixed, queer spaces where American rave took root.

Reference

Porcelain: A Memoir — Moby (2016). Moby's memoir: New York, rave and poverty.
