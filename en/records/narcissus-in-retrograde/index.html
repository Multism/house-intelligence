Why this record

Between melodic sweetness and industrial techno.

How it was made

Four tracks: "Natural Impasse", "Dystopian Daddy", "Why Does It Hurt" and "Groundwater". Kompakt-style sweetness on one side, the industrial monochrome of "Why Does It Hurt" on the other.
