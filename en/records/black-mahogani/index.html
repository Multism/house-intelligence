How it was made

A slow, soulful deep house album, between samples, played instruments and an end-of-night atmosphere.

SamplerInstruments live

Origins and production approach

Moodymann (Kenny Dixon Jr.), a Detroit producer and head of the KDJ label, released the album on Peacefrog in May 2004, after a run of rare 12"s.

Club sociology

It is one of the most revered works of Detroit's second wave, the one that claims the city's soul and Black culture.
