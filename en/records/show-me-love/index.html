Why this record

A single preset colour is enough to define a decade.

How it was made

StoneBridge's remix kept only the vocal and kick drum of the original. Moving to the next preset on his Korg M1, "Organ 2", he found the organ bass that became one of the signatures of the '90s.

Korg M1 Music Workstation

Origins and production approach

Robin S. (Robin Jackson Maynard) came from R&B and pop. The original version, produced by Allen George and Fred McFarlane, came out on Champion in October 1990 and flopped; the demo had been sung by Andrea Martin. Swedish remixer StoneBridge turned it into a club hit: transatlantic circulation at full power.

Club sociology

Mixed in 1992 and released in 1993, the remix reached number 6 in the UK, number 5 in the US and number one on US club charts. It has been sampled or reworked for thirty years, from Jason Derulo to Charli XCX and Beyoncé's "Break My Soul".

Ballroom & queer scenes

The gospel diva voice par excellence for gay dancefloors and ballroom.
