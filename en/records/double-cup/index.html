Why this record

It extends the Chicago line beyond 2005.

How it was made

Triplet kicks around 160 BPM, chopped vocal samples and softer pads than battle footwork: footwork turned towards listening.

SamplerBoîte à rythmes (808)

Origins and production approach

DJ Rashad, a leading figure of the Teklife crew, came out of Chicago ghetto house (Dance Mania, DJ Funk). He died in 2014, a year after the album came out.

Club sociology

The album came out on Hyperdub in London: footwork, born from South Side and West Side dance battles, found an international audience.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis: footwork is first a dance culture of Chicago's Black youth.
