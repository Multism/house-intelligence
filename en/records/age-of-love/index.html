Why this record

The first trance record in the modern sense.

How it was made

A hypnotic female voice, a rolling 909 and spiralling arpeggios. Jam & Spoon's remix sets trance's grammar: build, break, lift-off.

Synthétiseurs (arpèges)Roland TR-909 Rhythm Composer

Origins and production approach

An Italo-Belgian project (Bruno Sanchioni). The remix by Frankfurt duo Jam & Spoon turned a club song into a trance prototype.

Club sociology

Trance emerged in the early 90s between German clubs (Omen and Dorian Gray in Frankfurt, Tresor in Berlin) and British and Ibizan dancefloors. Hypnotised traces this European rise, all the way to the big Dutch gatherings.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer anchoring. Trance builds a collective utopia of euphoria rather than a community identity.

Reference

Hypnotised: A Journey Through Trance Music (1990–2005) — Arjan Rietveld (2022). The beginnings of trance and progressive house in Europe.
