Why this record

Toronto house, understated and full of references.

How it was made

Four tracks: "Hundred Flowers Groove", "Blo A Wish", "The Twirler" and a Central remix. Understated and groovy, led by nimble drum programming and soft-focus samples; the EP nods to 1956 China and My Bloody Valentine.

Boîtes à rythmesSamplesSynthés

Origins and production approach

Ciel is Cindy Li, a Toronto producer. Hundred Flowers follows Electrical Encounters (Peach Discs, 2017).

Club sociology

"The Twirler" pays tribute to a Toronto dancefloor figure. The EP came out on Coastal Haze, Seb Wildblood and Hollick's London label.
