Why this record

Proof that Chicago house could be tender and sensual.

How it was made

An endlessly cycling synth arpeggio, a minimal bassline and Jamie Principle's sensual, almost whispered vocal. A record of night and desire, the opposite of frantic jack.

Synthétiseur (arpège)Boîte à rythmes

Origins and production approach

Jamie Principle wrote an electronic love song at home. Frankie Knuckles played it from a reel-to-reel tape at the Power Plant for years before its official release.

Club sociology

The exclusive tape, played by one DJ only, is a model of Chicago club economics: the DJ is the first broadcaster, the record comes later.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Knuckles, the "Godfather of House", was a Black gay DJ from the Bronx and the Continental Baths. "Your Love" is one of the founding love songs of queer house.

Reference

Do You Remember House? Chicago's Queer of Color Undergrounds — Micah E. Salkind (2019). Frankie Knuckles and Chicago's queer-of-color undergrounds.
