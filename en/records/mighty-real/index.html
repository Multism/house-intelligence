Why this record

"Mighty Real" is a manifesto: queer fervour as the engine of the dancefloor.

How it was made

A relentless kick, piercing synths added by Patrick Cowley and Sylvester's gospel falsetto on top. Hi-NRG before the name: faster, more electronic and more ecstatic than New York disco.

Synthétiseurs (Patrick Cowley)Section rythmique

Origins and production approach

Sylvester James, raised in a Los Angeles Pentecostal church, landed in San Francisco's drag and countercultural scene (the Cockettes). Patrick Cowley, engineer and synthesist, became his technical alter ego.

Club sociology

The track ruled the Castro's clubs and became an international hit. San Francisco built its own gay circuit of electronic disco, parallel to New York.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Openly gay, Black and gender-nonconforming, Sylvester is disco's most explicitly queer figure. Cowley died of AIDS in 1982, Sylvester in 1988: their story is also the epidemic's.

Reference

Hot Stuff: Disco and the Remaking of American Culture — Alice Echols (2010). The chapter on Sylvester and San Francisco gay disco.
