Why this record

Trance as the soundtrack of reunification.

How it was made

Bright major chords, a synth melody carried by an arpeggiated bass: euphoric Berlin trance.

SynthétiseursSéquenceur

Origins and production approach

Paul van Dyk grew up in East Berlin, where Western music arrived secretly over the radio. The fall of the Wall opened the clubs to him.

Club sociology

Reunified Berlin became a capital of the night thanks to the East's empty buildings. The Love Parade drew hundreds of thousands.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Berlin's scene has strong queer roots, but late-90s stadium trance drifted away from them.

Rave On: Global Adventures in Electronic Dance Music — Matthew Collin (2018). Berlin after the Wall.
