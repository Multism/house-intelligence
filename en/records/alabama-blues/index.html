Why this record

The other French Touch: deep, jazzy and hushed.

How it was made

A supple deep-house groove, blues samples and African-American voices, jazz pads: the jazz side of Paris house, far from the disco filter.

SamplerSamples de blues

Origins and production approach

Ludovic Navarre, self-taught and from the Paris suburbs, released on F Communications, Laurent Garnier and Éric Morand's label. He approached house as a jazz and blues lover.

Club sociology

The album Boulevard became a home-listening success as much as a club one, opening the way for a "lounge" house heard in bars and flats.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis. The record quotes Black American music as an archive, a recurring question for the French scene.

Reference

Musique house — Sean Bidder. The French Touch according to Bidder: Garnier, Dimitri from Paris, Ludovic Navarre.
