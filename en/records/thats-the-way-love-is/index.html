Why this record

House's soul heritage in full light.

How it was made

Byron Stingily's falsetto, soul strings and a deep groove: Philadelphia soul reread by Chicago.

Boîte à rythmesCordes (synthétiseur)

Origins and production approach

Ten City, with producer Marshall Jefferson, championed a "musical" house of singers, harmonies and strings, against the dryness of jack.

Club sociology

The group signed to Atlantic: a major betting on soulful house. The start of the "vocal deep house" that would inspire UK garage.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Stingily's falsetto extends soul's tradition of high male voices (Sylvester, Curtis Mayfield), blurring gender codes.

Reference

House Music… The Real Story — Jesse Saunders (with James Cummins) (2007). Chicago house on major labels in the late 1980s.
