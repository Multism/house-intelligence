Why this record

The other half of the 2013 crossover.

How it was made

A garage-and-house rhythm, a round bassline and Aluna Francis's pop vocal: 90s garage rewritten for radio.

Origins and production approach

Guy and Howard Lawrence, two brothers from Surrey, signed to PMR; they brought in the London duo AlunaGeorge.

Club sociology

The single reached number 2 in the UK in February 2013, two months before Duke Dumont's number 1: house returns to the top of the charts.
