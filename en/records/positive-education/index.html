Origins and production approach

Stuart McMillan and Orde Meikle, residents at Glasgow's Sub Club, founded Soma in 1991 with Dave Clarke. "Positive Education" was its eighth release. They would go on to sign Daft Punk's first record.

Club sociology

Slam's residency was the Saturday night at Glasgow's Sub Club. Its link with the Arches, the vaults under Central Station whose story Brickwork tells up to their 2015 closure, still needs confirming in the book.

Ballroom & queer scenes

The Scottish scene was mostly straight. The Arches also hosted theatre and alternative nights, though, making it more open than a standard club.
