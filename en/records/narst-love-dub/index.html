Why this record

London house between UK funky and dubstep.

How it was made

Cooly G's first 12" on Hyperdub: "Narst", mock-aggressive, and "Love Dub", atmospheric and measured, followed by a "Refix" with a heavier kick. Tribal conga rolls under crooner vocals.

DAWPercussions

Origins and production approach

Cooly G lives in Brixton. In 2009 she was a semi-professional footballer as well as a DJ and producer on the UK funky scene. She came from house, which she mixes with UK house.

Club sociology

Hyperdub, Kode9's label, signed her while the press called her the label's "first lady": a woman producer in a very male bass scene.
