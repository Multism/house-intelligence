Why this record

Deep house, Hamburg style: slow, melodic, autumnal.

How it was made

Debut album, nine tracks ("A Line", "Do Little", "The Cool World", "Always the Same", "Ne Travaillez Jamais"…). Rhodes, or keyboards that sound like it, everywhere, and melodies that unfold very slowly.

Piano & orgueSynthés

Origins and production approach

Christopher Rau is a Hamburg producer. Before this album he had released only a few 12"s, on Gedankensport and Giegling.

Club sociology

Resident Advisor heard it less as deep house than as "autumnal house". The record takes up Chicago and Detroit sounds with the restraint of German minimal; "Ne Travaillez Jamais", released in 2009, was already a favourite of the Dial label's DJs.
