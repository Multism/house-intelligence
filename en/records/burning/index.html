Why this record

The other French house, the diggers' one.

How it was made

Three tracks ("Burning Hot", "The Right Way", "Deep Burnt") built on chopped, filtered samples. "Deep Burnt", on the B-side, became the best-known track.

SamplerFiltres

Origins and production approach

Pépé Bradock, a Parisian producer, released this 12" in 1999 on Kif Recordings. He later founded his own label, Atavisme.

Club sociology

It is a classic of French deep house, repressed several times (again in 2018). It shows a Parisian house that owes more to jazz and soul than to the French touch filter.
