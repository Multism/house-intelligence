Why this record

Hypnotic, acid house, from Tokyo to Berlin.

How it was made

Four tracks, all starting with the EP's initial: "Hip", "Heart", "Hole" and "Hair". Pumping spurts of acid on the opener, dank industrial rhythms elsewhere.

Origins and production approach

Powder is a Japanese DJ and producer. Resident Advisor highlights her versatility, as a DJ and in the studio.

Club sociology

The EP came out on CockTail d'Amore, the label of a Berlin queer party: a Japanese scene circulating through European queer networks.
