Why this record

A DJ who became an icon, and another Black genealogy of house.

How it was made

A DJ single with a proto-hardcore sound, euphoric pads and a sampled vocal, built for Britain's big raves.

BreakbeatsSynthés

Origins and production approach

Carl Cox, son of Barbadian immigrants, grew up in south London. A sound-system and wedding DJ, he became one of the first Black British superstar DJs, famous for mixing on three decks.

Club sociology

In his autobiography, Cox describes the path of a Black DJ through an often white British industry, from illegal raves to his Ibiza residencies.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Cox embodies another lineage of house: Britain's Caribbean sound systems more than queer clubs.
