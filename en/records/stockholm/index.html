Origins and production approach

The Persuader is an alias of Jesper Dahlbäck, born in Stockholm in 1974. The album came out in 1999 on Svek, Stephan Grieder's label, active from 1996 to 2003.

Club sociology

An archival detail: its vinyl edition is the very first entry in the Discogs database, release no. 1.
