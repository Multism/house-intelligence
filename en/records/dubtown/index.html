Why this record

The Wiggle sound on record: London tech house meets dub.

How it was made

Four tracks ("Hannah's House", "Hannah's Dub", "As You Cry", "Reggae's House") in which London tech house borrows dub's echoes and basslines.

Boîtes à rythmesDelay

Origins and production approach

Terry Francis, a British DJ and producer born in 1966, is a pillar of the Wiggle parties with Nathan Coles, with whom he also produces as Housey Doingz. "Dubtown" is his first solo record. Muzik named him Best New DJ in 1997, and he became a fabric resident when it opened in 1999.

Club sociology

The record is a landmark of the London tech house of the Wiggle parties. Mint Condition has reissued it, remastered with Francis.
