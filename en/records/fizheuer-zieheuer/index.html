Why this record

Minimal at its most extreme.

How it was made

Thirty-seven minutes built on a single brass loop and a minimal rhythm that barely changes: repetition pushed to the point of hypnosis.

OrdinateurSamples de cuivres

Origins and production approach

Ricardo Villalobos, born in Chile and brought to Germany as a child, is a leading figure of Berlin minimal; the EP came out in November 2006 on Playhouse, a Frankfurt label.

Club sociology

The record polarised listeners: a masterpiece for some, an excess for others. It embodies the minimal of Berlin clubs, where sets last for hours.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis for the record; Berlin's after-hours scene mixes gay and straight crowds.

Reference

Energy Flash: A Journey Through Rave Music and Dance Culture — Simon Reynolds (1998). Simon Reynolds cites this record among those still pushing dance music into new territory (expanded edition).
