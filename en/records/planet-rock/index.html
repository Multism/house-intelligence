Why this record

A decisive link: electro connects Kraftwerk, hip-hop and the techno to come.

How it was made

The melody of Kraftwerk's "Trans-Europe Express" and the beat of "Numbers", replayed, a TR-808 pushing the low end, and rap vocals thrown out like a party call. Kraftwerk was not credited at first; a settlement followed.

Roland TR-808 Rhythm ComposerSynthétiseurs (Arthur Baker, John Robie)

Origins and production approach

Afrika Bambaataa came from the Bronx, a former gang leader who founded the Universal Zulu Nation. Arthur Baker and John Robie, Tommy Boy producers, built the record.

Club sociology

The Bronx of block parties and downtown New York clubs mixed hip-hop, disco and new wave. Planet Rock's electro opened the way for Detroit's, of which Cybotron would be one of the first echoes.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis in the record itself. To be framed through downtown clubs (Paradise Garage, Danceteria) where electro circulated.
