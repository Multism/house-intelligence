Why this record

Here drag is not décor, it is the text of the track itself.

How it was made

A dark, hypnotic tribal groove, a growling bass and Celeda's spoken word: "Music is the answer to your problems". The track builds as continuous tension, with no chorus.

Roland TR-909 Rhythm ComposerPercussions samplées

Origins and production approach

Danny Tenaglia, from Queens, grew up listening to Larry Levan. He inherited garage's marathon culture: his sets often ran past ten hours.

Club sociology

Tenaglia's "Be Yourself" parties at Vinyl and his nights at Twilo formed one of the last great communities of New York clubbing, before the Giuliani administration's "quality of life" policy closed clubs one by one.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Celeda (Victoria Sharpe), a Chicago-born singer and drag performer, carries the text: her voice turns a tribal track into a sermon, placing drag at the very heart of the production.

Reference

Last Night a DJ Saved My Life — Bill Brewster & Frank Broughton (1999). Larry Levan's legacy among 1990s New York DJs.
