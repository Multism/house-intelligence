Why this record

The album that turned minimal into auteur work.

How it was made

Long tracks with dubby, winding percussion, where rhythm turns into melody. "Easy Lee" starts from a vocoded voice and becomes a long minimal funk groove.

PercussionsDAWVoix

Origins and production approach

Ricardo Villalobos, born in Chile and brought to Germany as a child, released this debut album on 19 September 2003 on Playhouse, a Frankfurt label, seven years after his first 12".

Club sociology

Resident Advisor ranked it the number one album of the 2000s. Villalobos then became one of the figures of Berlin's long minimal nights.
