Why this record

The link between "The Ha Dance" and Renaissance.

How it was made

The "ha" and the crash inherited from "The Ha Dance", a tense rhythm and the chants of ballroom commentator Kevin JZ Prodigy: a track made for the categories.

DAWSamples (« The Ha Dance »)

Origins and production approach

MikeQ, a New Jersey producer, released the track on the Let It All Out EP (Fade to Mind, 2011). He dates Qween Beat back to 2005, first as the name he released his music under, then as a collective; the label's first official release, Qweendom, came out in August 2016.

Club sociology

The track was sampled in 2022 by Beyoncé on "Pure/Honey" (Renaissance): ballroom moves from a closed scene to a pop reference.

Ballroom & queer scenes

This is the music of contemporary balls, made by and for Black and Latina queer and trans houses.

Reference

Paris Is Burning — Jennie Livingston (1990). The ballroom context this beat extends.
