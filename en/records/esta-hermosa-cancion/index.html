Why this record

Chicago's jazzy deep house, played by hand, in the middle of the minimal era.

How it was made

A light, percussive track with melodic keys and a live solo by Glenn Underground. The flip is more Latin-inspired.

PercussionsInstruments live

Origins and production approach

Abicah Soul is the alias of Chicago's DJ Stax. Glenn Underground, a figure of Chicago's jazzy deep house (Strictly Jaz Unit with Boo Williams, Peacefrog, Guidance), learned to DJ from his uncle and taught himself production. The 12" came out in May 2003 on Moods & Grooves (MG 021), a Detroit deep house label run by Mike Grant.

Club sociology

This 12" connects Chicago and Detroit at a time when Black American deep house circulated mostly through vinyl and European record shops.
