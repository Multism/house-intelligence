Why this record

Japan in the story of machines, before house.

How it was made

Programmed sequences and cold synth pads: Tokyo as a machine-city. The group acknowledges its debt to Kraftwerk while adding Japanese pop writing.

Roland MC-8 MicrocomposerSynthétiseurs modulaires

Origins and production approach

Haruomi Hosono, Ryuichi Sakamoto and Yukihiro Takahashi formed YMO; Hideki Matsutake programmed the machines.

Club sociology

YMO's records circulated in New York electro and are often listed among the influences of Detroit's generation. Japan appears here as a producer of sounds, not just a receiver.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis. The record is included for the genealogy of machines.
