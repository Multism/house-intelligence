Why this record

It shows jungle comes from the Jamaican sound system.

How it was made

Chopped breaks, a huge reggae bassline and General Levy's dancehall toasting: ragga jungle at its most popular.

Breakbeats (Amen)Basse reggae

Origins and production approach

M-Beat, a young London producer, and General Levy, a dancehall MC, brought jungle and Jamaican sound-system culture together.

Club sociology

A UK top 10 in 1994, it sparked a row within the scene over jungle's commercialisation.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Ragga jungle also inherited dancehall's masculinity; it carries no queer axis.

Reference

State of Bass: The Origins of Jungle/Drum & Bass — Martin James (1997). Ragga jungle and the controversies of 1994.
