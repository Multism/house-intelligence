Why this record

House slowed down until it became listening music.

How it was made

Fourteen tracks blending Parisian jazz, IDM, slowed-down house, classical music, musique concrète and hip-hop. Jaar uses his own voice as texture; "I Got A" is built around a Ray Charles vocal.

DAWSamplerVoix

Origins and production approach

Nicolas Jaar, Chilean-born and New York-based, released his first 12" at 17 ("The Student", Wolf & Lamb, 2008). Space Is Only Noise is his debut album, on the Parisian label Circus Company.

Club sociology

A slow house record that reached an audience far beyond the clubs. It opened the way for listening house, closer to the album than the 12".
