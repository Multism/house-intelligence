Why this record

Rhythmically inventive house, between Chicago and bass music.

How it was made

Three tracks: "Organ Smith", "About Finding The Right Balance" and "Uchi's House". A Cajmere-style organ loop amid bells and gasps, then a nervous hybrid leaning towards UK bass music.

OrguePercussionsSynthés

Origins and production approach

rRoxymore is Hermione Frank, from Montpellier. She first DJed in Paris before moving to Berlin, and has worked with Planningtorock and Paula Temple.

Club sociology

The 12" came out on Don't Be Afraid, a Bristol label: rRoxymore's house moves between Berlin and the UK scene.
