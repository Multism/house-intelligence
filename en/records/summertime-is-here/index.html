Why this record

The Sound Signature station comes alive.

How it was made

A sampled soul loop, filtered and left to breathe for more than ten minutes, with a tempo that seems to float. The grit is deliberately raw.

SamplerFiltres

Origins and production approach

Theo Parrish, born in Washington D.C. in 1972, raised in Chicago and based in Michigan from 1994, founded Sound Signature in 1997.

Club sociology

Released as a 12" on Sound Signature in 1999 (SS008), the track became a classic for deep DJs; it has been repressed several times and included on the Parallel Dimensions compilation.
