Why this record

A Brooklyn party becomes a label, with a raw, warm first 12".

How it was made

Three tracks, "Mad Disrespect", "Slackness" and "Tusk": percussion inherited from New York garage, woozy Detroit-style synths, interlocking drum and vocal loops. Made on a computer, without a tape machine.

DAWSampler

Origins and production approach

Anthony Naples was born in Miami and moved to New York around 2010, where he worked at Captured Tracks. This is his first record, and the first on the Mister Saturday Night label.

Club sociology

Mister Saturday Night was first a Brooklyn party, run by Justin Carter and Eamon Harkin, before it became a label. The record launched its catalogue; Four Tet then asked Naples for a remix.
