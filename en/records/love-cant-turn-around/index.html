Why this record

House's first crossover into Europe.

How it was made

A bouncing bassline and piano stabs. Above all, Darryl Pandy's operatic gospel voice, pushing the song all the way to a scream.

Roland TR-808 Rhythm ComposerPiano

Origins and production approach

Farley Keith Williams was one of the original Hot Mix 5 DJs on WBMX: radio was his stage. The track reworks Isaac Hayes's "I Can't Turn Around", already covered by Steve "Silk" Hurley; Farley made his own version with Jesse Saunders, with lyrics by Vince Lawrence. Hurley saw it as the theft of his own production: a typical Chicago credit dispute.

Club sociology

It reached number 10 on the UK singles chart in 1986: Chicago house entered the European charts even before the acid explosion.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Editorial reading, to be sourced: Darryl Pandy's flamboyant voice extends house's tradition of gospel singers. His place in the genre's queer history still needs documenting through interviews.
