Why this record

The Montevideo scene, seen from one of its clubs.

How it was made

Four tracks: "Sendas", "Basement", "S&T" and "PTN2". A live-sounding bassline, a whistling melody, a short robotic vocal.

Origins and production approach

Z@P is a Uruguayan producer with ties to Montevideo's Phonotheque club.

Club sociology

In the 2010s Montevideo, around the Phonotheque, became a hub of minimal house and electro recognised in Europe.
