Why this record

Where disco turns dub, experimental and deeply queer.

How it was made

Loose percussion, a dub groove and a female vocal drifting above. The "female vocal" version mixed by Larry Levan became the Paradise Garage reference.

Percussions liveVoix

Origins and production approach

Loose Joints was a project of Arthur Russell, a composer and cellist from New York's avant-garde, with DJ Steve D'Acquisto.

Club sociology

The record circulated in clubs before release. Levan and D'Acquisto carried the version through the Black and Latino gay circuit as the Paradise Garage rose.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Arthur Russell was gay; his music links the downtown avant-garde and the dance floor. A rare bridge between those two scenes.

Reference

The Disco Files 1973–78: New York's Underground, Week by Week — Vince Aletti (2009). Context of New York clubs in 1980.
