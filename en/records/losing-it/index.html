Why this record

Chosen for what it reveals: house turned into a global mass industry.

How it was made

A minimal rolling bassline, a looped vocal that rises ("losing it"), then a drop. The structure is built for collective explosion and viral festival clips.

DAWBasse roulante (sound design)

Origins and production approach

Paul Fisher, a former Australian pro surfer, has a carefree, humorous approach: the social-media DJ-showman.

Club sociology

Nominated for the Grammy for Best Dance Recording, the track marks the moment tech house replaced EDM as the dominant sound of festivals and Ibiza's megaclubs.

Ballroom & queer scenes

The antithesis of the Warehouse: festival tech house is often described as "bro", masculine and straight. It measures how far house has travelled from its origins.

Reference

The Underground Is Massive: How Electronic Dance Music Conquered America — Michaelangelo Matos (2015). Dance music turned festival industry.
