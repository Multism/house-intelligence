Origins and production approach

Boo Williams has made house in Chicago since 1994. With Glenn Underground he formed the core of Strictly Jaz Unit. The album came out in 1996 on Relief Records, a Cajual sub-label, and was reissued in 2011 on Another Day.

Club sociology

The record belongs to Chicago's second wave, which kept local house going once the spotlight had moved to the UK, and which Europe discovered mostly through imports.
