Why this record

An intimate house album, before the queer scene Octo Octa would help build.

How it was made

Eight tracks ("Who I Will Become", "Bad Blood", "Please Don't Leave", "Come Closer", "His Kiss", "Work Me", "Uneasy", "Fear"): polished re-readings of classic house, iridescent synths, sensual a cappellas, and dub techno synths on "Fear".

Origins and production approach

Octo Octa is Maya Bouldry-Morrison, born in Chicago in 1987. In 2013 she lived in Brooklyn and released music on 100% Silk, a Not Not Fun sub-label. She cites DJ Sprinkles among her influences.

Club sociology

100% Silk was then releasing house that came from the American DIY scene rather than from clubs. Octo Octa would become one of the figures of queer house in the 2010s and 2020s.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Octo Octa came out as trans in 2016. In 2019 she founded the T4T LUV NRG label with her partner, the DJ Eris Drew.
