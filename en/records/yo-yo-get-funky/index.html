Why this record

A precious document of how house negotiated its identity in the face of hip-hop.

How it was made

A four-to-the-floor house kick, sampled horn stabs and a rap flow that exhorts the floor. The MC replaces the diva; command replaces supplication.

Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer

Origins and production approach

Fast Eddie Smith, a DJ in WBMX's Hot Mix 5, came from radio and mixing. Hip-house was an attempt to put Chicago's two dominant Black musics in dialogue.

Club sociology

In the late '80s Chicago labels (DJ International, Trax) were looking for a commercial second wind, and major labels backed hip-house. The record entered the UK singles chart in March 1989 (number 54).

Ballroom & queer scenes

Hip-house leaned on rap's masculinity to widen house's audience beyond its queer crowds. You can read it as an attempt to "de-queer" the Chicago sound, even if the border stayed porous.

Reference
