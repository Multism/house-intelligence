Why this record

Proof that acid never died.

How it was made

A single 303 line whose filter keeps climbing, over a dry kick: tension is the subject of the record. It recalls Phuture's Acid Tracks eight years on.

Roland TB-303 Bass Line

Origins and production approach

Josh Wink comes from Philadelphia, where he co-founded the Ovum label with King Britt. The track first came out in March 1995 on New York's Strictly Rhythm, putting acid back in the spotlight while vocal house dominated.

Club sociology

The record was a worldwide club hit in 1995, including in the UK where the 1988 acid memory was still alive.

Ballroom & queer scenes

As with Acid Tracks, the lineage goes back to Chicago's Music Box, a Black and gay scene that rave narratives erased.

Reference

The Underground Is Massive: How Electronic Dance Music Conquered America — Michaelangelo Matos (2015). The acid revival in the US.
