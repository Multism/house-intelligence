Why this record

An untitled 12" that launched the Workshop aesthetic.

How it was made

Three untitled tracks (A, B1, B2), like all the label's early 12"s: stripped-down deep house built to last.

Origins and production approach

Move D is the Heidelberg producer David Moufang, who also releases music on his label Source Records. The record is credited with DJ Laté.

Club sociology

Workshop is a Berlin label whose 12"s have untitled tracks: people look for them by number, in specialist record shops and in DJ sets.
