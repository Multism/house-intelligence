Why this record

You feel the shift as soon as you mix it after a 121 BPM Chicago track: faster, yet looser.

How it was made

Two-step: the kick skips beats, the snare is syncopated, the hi-hats shuffle and the bass bounces. Craig David's R&B vocal dances over the rhythm. The high tempo never stops the groove from feeling loose.

SamplerTime-stretch vocal

Origins and production approach

Mark Hill and Pete Devereux (Artful Dodger) came from the southern English garage scene. They inherited speed garage and the chopped vocals of Todd Edwards (New Jersey).

Club sociology

UK garage was the music of Sunday sessions, pirate radio and MCs, with a sharp dress code (Moschino, champagne). A Black British working-class culture that would give birth to grime and dubstep, and that police would target through Form 696.

Ballroom & queer scenes

UK garage inherited New York garage, gospel vocals and soul, without taking on its queer spaces. Its queer memory is musical rather than social.

Reference
