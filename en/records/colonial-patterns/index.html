Why this record

House damaged until it becomes landscape.

How it was made

Debut album, fourteen tracks closer to ambient collage than to the club: slow tempos, tarnished textures, a one-bar bassline looping endlessly on "Ragtime U.S.A. (Warning)".

SamplerBouclesSaturation

Origins and production approach

Huerco S. is Brian Leeds, from Kansas. The album came out on Software, Daniel Lopatin's (Oneohtrix Point Never) label.

Club sociology

Critics filed the record under "outsider house", a label coined by the press. Resident Advisor heard in it "a sort of corrupted pastoral", rural and drowsy.
