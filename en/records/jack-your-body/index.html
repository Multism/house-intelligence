Why this record

The moment jack conquered the charts.

How it was made

A hammering bassline, a mechanical kick and a sampled, stuttering voice ("jack-jack-jack your body"). Jack in its purest form: an order addressed to the body.

Boîte à rythmesSampler

Origins and production approach

Steve Hurley was a radio and club DJ. The track grew out of tape edits made for his sets before it was pressed.

Club sociology

The first house number one in the UK, in January 1987, with barely any lyrics. Britain discovered house was a mass phenomenon.

Ballroom & queer scenes

"Jack" is a dance and a sexuality of the dancefloor: a vocabulary born in Chicago's Black gay clubs that the British charts imported without knowing it.
