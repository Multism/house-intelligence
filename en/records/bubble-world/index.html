Why this record

Vancouver invents its own house, between deep house and downtempo.

How it was made

Three tracks, "Bubble World", "Love Theme (Angelo)" and "Temple Walk": airy house between deep house and downtempo, the darker side of Mood Hut according to Dummy.

SynthésBoîtes à rythmes

Origins and production approach

Pender Street Steppers is a Vancouver duo, one half of which is Jack Jutson (Jack J).

Club sociology

Mood Hut is a Vancouver collective with its studios in Chinatown. It first released music on cassette, then on vinyl: a warm local scene that quickly found an international audience.
