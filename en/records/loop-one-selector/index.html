Why this record

The first release on a label founded by a producer.

How it was made

Two tracks, "Loop One" and "Selector": a straight house beat, chattering vocal samples and a hands-in-the-air synth riff; the B-side is tougher and bass-heavy.

Origins and production approach

Shanti Celeste is a Bristol DJ and producer. This 12" is the first release on her own label, Peach Discs, which she describes as the main home for her music.

Club sociology

A producer who founded her own label to release her music and that of other Bristol artists: transmission also happens through structures.
