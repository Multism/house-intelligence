Why this record

Deep house as a spiritual practice of the diaspora.

How it was made

A double album of spiritual deep house in which Afro-Latin percussion, keys and voices (including Wunmi on "Rader Du") take precedence over the machines. Titles borrow from Yoruba vocabulary ("Oxossi Da Focha Branca", "2thousandcowries").

Instruments livePercussionsVoix

Origins and production approach

Osunlade, born in St. Louis, Missouri, in 1969, composed for Sesame Street and worked with Patti LaBelle before founding Yoruba Records in New York. He is a priest of the Yoruba religion of Ifá. "Paradigm" came out in 2001 on London's Soul Jazz label (SJRLP52).

Club sociology

The record belongs to New York's early-2000s "spiritual" deep house, which links house to the musics and religions of the African diaspora.
