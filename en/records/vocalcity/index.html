Why this record

Proof that microhouse could sing: vocal house built with the tools of the computer.

How it was made

Six long tracks (10 to 16 minutes) in which fragments of voice, bass and chords are cut up, delayed and stitched back together. The vocal on "Market" only arrives after the fifth minute: the album takes deep house's time.

Origins and production approach

Luomo is the house alias of Sasu Ripatti, a Finnish producer already known for the dub ambient of Vladislav Delay. The album came out in May 2000 on Force Tracks (FT14), a sub-label of Frankfurt's Force Inc. network. Singer Johanna Iivanainen lends her voice.

Club sociology

A reference album of microhouse: Resident Advisor ranked it the 13th best album of the 2000s and Pitchfork gave it 9.7/10 in 2005. Ripatti nonetheless said in 2010 it was his least favourite album.
