Origins and production approach

Swayzak, the London duo of David Brown and James S. Taylor, released this debut album in March 1998, on Pagan in the UK and The Medicine Label in the US. The title names the dream holiday they would take if the album did well.

Club sociology

Together with Himawari (2000), the album places Swayzak between British tech house and German minimal.
