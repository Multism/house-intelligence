Why this record

Techno's declaration of independence.

How it was made

A sequenced bassline, vocoded voices and an 808 electro beat. More funk than house, but it lays down techno's machine grammar.

Roland TR-808 Rhythm ComposerSynthétiseurs

Origins and production approach

Juan Atkins founded his own label, Metroplex, to control his output: independence sits at the core of Detroit techno's ethic.

Club sociology

One of Detroit's first "techno" releases. Specialist shops imported it into Europe before it was even known at home.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis. This is Detroit Afrofuturism: imagining an elsewhere for Black bodies.

Reference

Techno Rebels: The Renegades of Electronic Funk — Dan Sicko (1999). Metroplex and Detroit's independence.
