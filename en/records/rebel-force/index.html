Why this record

Melbourne, from the suburbs to a New York label.

How it was made

Ten tracks ("Red Rock (IV Mix)", "Central", "Danza Mart", "Reggatron", "Morning Sequence"…): ultra-polished dance tracks loaded with melody, with a belching bass synth and pulsating arps.

Origins and production approach

Sleep D is the Melbourne duo of Corey Kikos and Maryos Syawish. They released their first record at 18, while living in suburban Frankston. They run Butter Sessions, first a blog, then a mix series, party and label.

Club sociology

A Melbourne album released in New York on Incienso: the Australian scene circulates through international underground networks.
