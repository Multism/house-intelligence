Why this record

The template for all deep house: emotion through chords, not effects.

How it was made

Warm pads, a round bassline and almost no variation for seven minutes. Depth comes from restraint. Later versions lay Chuck Roberts's sermon on top ("In the beginning there was Jack").

Roland TR-909 Rhythm ComposerRoland Juno-60

Origins and production approach

Larry Heard is a former jazz-fusion drummer. He composes as a harmonist, with seventh and ninth chords, giving house a jazz and soul vocabulary.

Club sociology

Deep house offered a counterpoint to jack frenzy: a floor where you could close your eyes. It also introduced the idea that house could be listened to at home.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Chuck Roberts's text, often mixed over it, makes house a space of universal belonging ("house is a feeling"): a theology of inclusion born in Black gay clubs.
