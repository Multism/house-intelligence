Why this record

The archetype of the French filter: one technical gesture, repeated to ecstasy.

How it was made

A loop from Chaka Khan's "Fate" (1981) runs through a filter that opens and closes over a four-to-the-floor kick, with Benjamin Diamond's vocal on top. The whole track rests on the filter's breathing.

Origins and production approach

Thomas Bangalter, Alan Braxe and Benjamin Diamond released only one record under this name. An almost accidental studio project became a global hit.

Club sociology

Filter house turns disco into raw material: lift a few seconds from a 70s or 80s record and loop them. Crate digging becomes both an aesthetic and an economy, with all its rights issues.

Ballroom & queer scenes

This sampled disco is the sound archive of 1970s Black and gay dancefloors. Filter house recontextualises it for a wider, often straight audience: homage or appropriation, depending on how you hear it.

Reference

Last Night a DJ Saved My Life — Bill Brewster & Frank Broughton (1999). Disco as matrix and the status of the sample.
