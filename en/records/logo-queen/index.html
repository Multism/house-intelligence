Why this record

The first record of a Japanese label that became a digging reference.

How it was made

Three tracks, "Logo Queen", "Scan Runner" and "Selfless State": dreamy, melodic deep house built to roll for a long time in a set.

DAWSynthés

Origins and production approach

So Inagawa is a Japanese producer and DJ, co-founder of Cabaret Recordings with DJ Masda. Logo Queen is the label's first record.

Club sociology

Resident Advisor describes Cabaret as a Japanese label that puts a hypnotic twist on classic club sounds. Its records circulate mostly among DJs and minimal diggers.
