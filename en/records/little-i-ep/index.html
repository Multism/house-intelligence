Why this record

British tech house at its dreamiest.

How it was made

Four tracks ("New Life", "Seven Miles Up", "No Going Back", "Dream") with a relaxed, almost ambient atmosphere over a deep house groove.

SynthésNappes

Origins and production approach

Fresh & Low is the British trio of Julian Dembinski, David Robertson and Calum Walker, described as tech house pioneers. The EP came out in 1997 on West Side Records.

Club sociology

Long hard to find, the EP was reissued in 2026 by Rawax: an example of the 1990s British 12"s that diggers have put back into circulation.
