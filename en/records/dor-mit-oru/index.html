Why this record

The long time of Romanian minimal.

How it was made

Rhadoo's first 12" for Cadenza: four long tracks (from nine to over thirteen minutes), "Bau", "Baisbe Alt Bass", "Elan" and "Slagare". Austere house with treated percussion and tribal, folk-inspired patterns.

DAWPercussions

Origins and production approach

Rhadoo is a Bucharest DJ and producer, one of the three founders of [a:rpia:r] with Petre Inspirescu and Raresh.

Club sociology

The tracks are built for sets lasting several hours, where tension rises slowly. That is the hallmark of the Romanian scene, which critics at the time began to treat as a scene of its own.
