Why this record

The commercial peak of the French filter.

How it was made

A guitar riff sampled from Chic's "Soup for One", a disco kick and a pop vocal: the French Touch at its most radio-friendly.

SamplerGuitare (sample)

Origins and production approach

Romain Tranchart and Yann Destagnol, two music students, made a global hit on almost no budget, thanks to the sample.

Club sociology

A UK number one. The Chic sample, credited to Nile Rodgers and Bernard Edwards, is a reminder that disco literally feeds this economy.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Chic's disco, born in New York's mixed and gay clubs, becomes mainstream European pop.

Reference

Hot Stuff: Disco and the Remaking of American Culture — Alice Echols (2010). Disco as the matrix of pop.
