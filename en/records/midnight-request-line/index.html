Why this record

The Dubstep station comes alive.

How it was made

A haunting synth melody, deep sub-bass and a half-time rhythm at 140 BPM: dubstep at its most melodic.

DAWSub-bass

Origins and production approach

Skream (Oliver Jones), a Croydon teenager, orbited around Big Apple Records, the shop at the heart of dark garage and then dubstep.

Club sociology

The FWD>> and DMZ nights were the scene's key venues; this record marked its move to a worldwide audience.

Ballroom & queer scenes

No explicit queer axis: the dubstep scene is first of all a culture of bass and sound systems.

Reference

Energy Flash: A Journey Through Rave Music and Dance Culture — Simon Reynolds (1998). The hardcore continuum and its extensions.
