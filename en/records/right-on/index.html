Why this record

A successful junction of Detroit and Chicago, seen from Glasgow.

How it was made

A rolling bassline, dry percussion and a looped funk vocal sample. The rigour of techno meets the warmth of house, with no intrusive melody.

Origins and production approach

Graeme Reedie and Craig Morrison came out of Glasgow's Sub Club and Soma scene: a deeply rooted local club culture.

Club sociology

Tech house was born in London's warehouses and after-hours from 1994 onwards (the Wiggle parties of Terry Francis, Nathan Coles and Eddie Richards, Heart & Soul, Mr C and Layo's club The End). Long-night music, made for DJs.

Ballroom & queer scenes

British tech house grew up in mostly white, straight scenes. It illustrates how house drifted away from its Black queer origins as it became more "technical".
