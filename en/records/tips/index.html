Why this record

Romanian minimal leaves Bucharest.

How it was made

A double 12" of four tracks ("Mostra", "Rackadoom", "Galantar", "La Crème Bonjour"): slow, hypnotic house with chopped percussion and melodies reminiscent of the zither or harpsichord.

DAWPercussions

Origins and production approach

Petre Inspirescu is from Bucharest. With Rhadoo and Raresh he forms the trio behind the [a:rpia:r] label. "Tips" came out on the Cadenza label.

Club sociology

In the late 2000s Bucharest became one of the centres of European minimal: very long sets, tracks that often run past ten minutes, and a scene carried by a few DJs.
