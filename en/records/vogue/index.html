Why this record

The bridge between ballroom, house and global pop.

How it was made

A house piano line, a garage bassline and a spoken list of Hollywood icons. Shep Pettibone, a New York producer from the clubs, shaped a track made for the floor as much as for radio.

Boîte à rythmesSynthétiseurs (production Shep Pettibone)

Origins and production approach

Madonna had frequented downtown New York and gay club scenes since the 80s; Pettibone came from club remixing.

Club sociology

The record and video popularized vogue beyond Harlem. Dancers from the House of Xtravaganza joined Madonna's tour, and the film Paris Is Burning came out the same year.

Ballroom & queer scenes

The record is central to the appropriation debate: Black and Latino queer ballroom culture was shown to the world without its creators sharing the same benefit.
