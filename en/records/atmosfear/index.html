Why this record

Jazz enters Chicago deep house through the Rhodes and the Juno.

How it was made

Debut album: eight deep house tracks in which 1970s jazz-fusion chords sit on programmed beats ("Israelee Night Falls", "May Datroit", "Midnight Groove").

Piano & orgueSynthés

Origins and production approach

Glenn Underground grew up on Chicago's South Side. He learned to DJ from his uncle, taught himself production and co-founded Strictly Jaz Unit with Boo Williams. The London label Peacefrog released this debut album in 1996.

Club sociology

A British label released the album: in the 1990s, Chicago's Black deep house found much of its audience in Europe. Peacefrog has announced a 30th-anniversary reissue.
