Why this record

The bridge between balearic and trance.

How it was made

A Mediterranean melody, stacking arpeggios and a slow build. The title pays homage to the Ibiza bar where people watch the sunset.

SynthétiseursSéquenceur

Origins and production approach

Paul Schmitz-Moormann and Harald Blüchel (Cosmic Baby) worked for Eye Q, Sven Väth's Frankfurt label.

Club sociology

Remixed and reissued many times, it became an Ibiza club classic, linking German trance to balearic.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Ibiza, with its legacy of mixed hedonism, is the backdrop: the record itself has no queer axis.

Reference

Hypnotised: A Journey Through Trance Music (1990–2005) — Arjan Rietveld (2022). Ibiza and the golden age of European trance.
