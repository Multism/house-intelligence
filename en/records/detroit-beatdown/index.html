Why this record

Detroit slows down and claims its soul: beatdown gets its name.

How it was made

Fourteen previously unreleased tracks of slow, soulful deep house, between samples and played instruments: Malik Alston, Alton Miller, Theo Parrish ("Falling Up"), Rick Wilhite, Norm Talley, Mike Clark, Delano Smith, Eddie Fowlkes…

SamplerInstruments live

Origins and production approach

The compilation came out on 18 November 2002 on the British label Third Ear (3E 001CD), as a double CD with artwork by The Designers Republic. It gathers Detroit's second generation.

Club sociology

It gave a name, "beatdown", to a Detroit house that was slower, Blacker and more soulful than the exported techno. Third Ear founder Guy McCreery has explained the term.
