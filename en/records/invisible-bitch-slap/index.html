Why this record

New York returns to raw deep house, on producer-run labels.

How it was made

A two-track 12", "Invisible Bitch Slap" and "Woman Is The Devil": raw house between deep house and techno.

SynthésBoîtes à rythmes

Origins and production approach

Levon Vincent is an American producer. This record opened the Deconstruct Music catalogue (DEC-01); it was followed in 2009 by "Six Figures" on Novel Sound and "Solemn Days" on Deconstruct.

Club sociology

With Novel Sound and Deconstruct, a handful of New York producers put dark, minimalist deep house back on vinyl, just as European minimal dominated the clubs.
