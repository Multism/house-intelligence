Why this record

It lets you hear garage before house: an aesthetic of dub, space and fervour that runs through everything that follows.

How it was made

Drum-machine claps drowned in echo, a round bassline and Bernard Fowler's pleading vocal. The mix is conceived as dub: elements drop out and return, and space becomes part of the music.

Roland TR-808 Rhythm Composer

Origins and production approach

Larry Levan co-produced with Michael de Benedictus. Levan treated the studio as an extension of his booth: he mixed for the Paradise Garage sound system, not for radio.

Club sociology

The Paradise Garage at 84 King Street (1977–1987) was members-only, served no alcohol and ran until Sunday morning. Richard Long's sound and Levan's marathon sets made it a place of worship, people called it "Saturday Mass".

Ballroom & queer scenes

The Garage was one of the founding spaces of Black and Latino gay social life in New York. The AIDS epidemic hit it head-on; Levan died in 1992. "Garage" is first and foremost an archive of that community.
