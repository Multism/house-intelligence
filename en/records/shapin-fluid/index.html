Why this record

West London breaks the house kick and lets jazz in.

How it was made

Broken, syncopated rhythms, jazz keys and funk basslines: a broken beat album with guests from the scene, including Kaidi Tatham and Beaujolais.

Origins and production approach

Afronaught is the main alias of Orin Walters, one of the driving forces of West London's broken beat scene and a founding member of the Bugz in the Attic collective. He lived in Chicago in the mid-1990s. This debut album came out in 2001 on Apollo, an R&S sub-label.

Club sociology

Broken beat was born in West London's studios and clubs, a Black British scene mixing jazz, soul, house and drum and bass.
