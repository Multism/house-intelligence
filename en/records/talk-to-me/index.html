Why this record

It connects sample culture with internet irony.

How it was made

A pitched, looped vocal sample, chords bathed in hiss and a slightly off-grid groove. The timing and sonic imperfections are production choices.

SamplerSaturation

Origins and production approach

Felix Clary Weatherall picked an alias that jokes about the TV show Friends. Self-mockery is part of the aesthetic, the opposite of techno seriousness.

Club sociology

Lobster Theremin, an independent London label, structured this scene between limited vinyl and digital virality. The record became a collector's item for a post-streaming generation.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Like much lo-fi house, the track inherits a texture without claiming any link to the original queer scenes.

How Music Works — David Byrne (2012). Sound designed for home listening and platforms.
