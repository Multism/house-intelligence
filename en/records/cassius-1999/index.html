Why this record

It shows how porous rap and house were in Paris.

How it was made

A disco loop compressed to the extreme, a pumping filter and sampled vocals: the French Touch sound pushed into saturation.

SamplerFiltreCompresseur

Origins and production approach

Philippe Zdar (MC Solaar's engineer) and Hubert "Boombass" Blanc-Francard came from French rap: the French Touch is also a beatmakers' story.

Club sociology

The record reached number 7 in the UK singles chart in January 1999 and number 1 on the dance chart (Official Charts). The French Touch became an export industry funded by the majors.

Ballroom & queer scenes

Like filter house in general, it recycles queer disco without naming it.

Reference

Electrochoc — Laurent Garnier & David Brun-Lambert (2003). The late-90s French scene.
