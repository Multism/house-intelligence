Why this record

Deep house told in a whisper: the swing of minimal.

How it was made

Eleven swinging, unhurried microhouse cuts with discreet variations: hi-hats that move from closed to open, touches of jazz piano, a flute-like melody. "It's Coming" closes the album over nine minutes.

DAWPianoPercussions

Origins and production approach

Thomas Melchior, based in Berlin within the Perlon circle, started out on Trelik and Playhouse. With Baby Ford (Peter Adshead), he formed Soul Capsule, behind "Lady Science" (Trelik, 1999). "The Meaning" is his first solo album for Playhouse, in 2004.

Club sociology

The album belongs to the Perlon and Playhouse circle that turned minimal house into long, hypnotic afterparty music.
